Privacy policy

Ninja Swing
Developer: Vedran Milic
Android package: com.VedranMilic.NinjaSwing
Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy describes what Ninja Swing does with information on Android. The game does not ask you to create an account, and the developer does not run a server that receives your play data. Use of the game is covered by the terms of service.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few settings in local app storage on your phone. That storage stays on the device. It is not uploaded by the game. Uninstalling Ninja Swing deletes it.

  • Your best score
  • Whether the how-to-play hint has already been shown
  • Whether the day and night cycle is on
  • Music volume and sound-effects volume

Advertising

On Android, Ninja Swing shows interstitial ads with the Unity Ads SDK after some finished runs. The game does not show ads on the web build.

Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies, receives information from the device in order to request, show, and measure ads. That can include:

  • The Android advertising ID
  • IP address, which the ad network may use to infer a coarse location such as country or region
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, and the app package name
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or clicked

The developer does not receive a copy of the advertising ID or a profile of the ads you saw. Unity’s own policy describes how Unity uses that information: Unity game player and app user privacy policy.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings under Google settings, Ads, or Privacy. Those controls are provided by Android and Google, not by this game.

What this game does not collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos, microphone audio, or contacts
  • Payment details (the game has no in-app purchases)
  • An account, login, or cloud save

The developer does not sell personal information.

Children

Ninja Swing is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long information is kept

Settings listed above remain on the device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall Ninja Swing. Information collected by Unity Ads is kept by Unity under Unity’s privacy policy. The developer has no separate copy of that ad data to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will replace this one and the effective date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after the new date means the updated policy applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the Ninja Swing page in Google Play. The developer is Vedran Milic.
